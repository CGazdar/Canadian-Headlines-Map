Data

The numbers behind every chart on this site, free to download. One row per term, outlet and quarter.

Download headline_terms.csv

What each column means

ColumnMeaning
termThe word or phrase being counted in headlines
quarterCalendar quarter, for example 2023-Q2
outlet_id, outletShort code and full name of the news outlet
provinceProvince code, TER for the three territories combined, NAT for national outlets
leaning_groupleft, center or right, from Ground News ratings (Lean Left counts as left, Lean Right as right)
leaningThe outlet's exact rating: left, lean_left, center, lean_right or right
ownerCompany that owns the outlet
mentionsHeadlines that contain the term
headlines_analysedHeadlines from that outlet in that quarter after cleaning
rate_per_100kmentions per 100,000 headlines analysed

Outlet-quarters with fewer than 300 usable headlines are left out, so a missing row means missing data, not zero use. The file holds counts and rates only, with no headline text.
