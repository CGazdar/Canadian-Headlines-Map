Canadian headline terms, 2019 to now

How often a term appears in headlines, per 1,000, by province of the outlet. Click a province for its outlets over time, or “All of Canada” for the three leaning groups. Grey provinces have no usable data for the chosen period.

Rates are the average of each outlet's own rate, so large outlets do not dominate. Outlet-quarters with fewer than 300 usable headlines are left out, so gaps are missing data, not zero use. Colour shows the outlet's Ground News leaning (red = left/lean left, grey = center, blue = lean right/right). In “Leaning” mode a province takes the colour of the side whose outlets use the term most there; the deeper the colour, the bigger the lead. A province with outlets from only one side in the dataset takes that side's full colour, even though there is nothing to compare it with. Postmedia owns most blue outlets and shares content between them. “All of Canada” averages every outlet in the country by leaning group (Left includes Lean Left; Right includes Lean Right); click a province to see its individual outlets. Provinces with no outlet in the dataset (the Atlantic provinces and the territories) cannot be shown.
