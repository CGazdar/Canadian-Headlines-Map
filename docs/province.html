Local outlets

Each line is one outlet's rate per 1,000 headlines (colour = Ground News leaning: red = left/lean left, grey = center, blue = lean right/right). The dashed line is the average of every outlet in the country, for comparison. Outlet-quarters with fewer than 300 usable headlines are left out, so gaps in a line are missing data, not zero use. Postmedia papers share content, so several of them can move together.
