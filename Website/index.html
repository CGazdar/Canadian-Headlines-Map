Rates are the average of each outlet's own rate, so large outlets do not dominate. Outlet-quarters with fewer than 300 usable headlines are left out, so gaps are missing data, not zero use. Line colour is the outlet's Ground News leaning (blue = left/lean left, grey = center, red = lean right/right). Postmedia owns most red outlets and shares content between them. . Provinces with no outlet in the dataset (the Atlantic provinces and the territories) cannot be shown.
